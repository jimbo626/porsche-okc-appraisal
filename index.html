Selling or making an offer?
Here’s what to know.

Selling your vehicle

Is submitting the form a guaranteed offer?

No. It starts an appraisal conversation. Any preliminary offer is subject to inspection, ownership verification, and agreed purchase terms.

What if I still have a loan?

Let the team know. They can explain the payoff process and required documents. Any remaining balance affects the sale proceeds.

When do I get paid?

Confirm payment method and timing with the dealership. They depend on inspection, paperwork, title or lien requirements, and agreed terms.

What if the vehicle lookup does not work?

Choose manual entry and enter the year, make, and model. Lookup coverage varies and results may be incomplete.

Making your next move

Where does “Make Us an Offer” take me?

It opens B4 Auctions. Review the listing and use its available bidding or purchase options. Options vary by listing.

Can I see a vehicle before bidding?

Contact the selling dealership shown on the listing to confirm the location and arrange a visit or test drive, subject to availability.

What should I review before bidding?

Review condition details, reserve requirements, auction timing, fees, and payment terms. Ask the seller about anything that is unclear.

Can I finance a purchase or trade in a vehicle?

Discuss financing and trade-in options with the selling dealership before bidding. Availability and approval depend on the vehicle, dealership, and your circumstances.
